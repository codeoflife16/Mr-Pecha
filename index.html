A LETTER, JUST FOR YOU

Dear Hey You,

I don't really know where to begin, because there are so many little things I want to tell you. First of all, thank you for being the reason behind so many of my smiles. You probably have absolutely no idea how often you make me smile. Sometimes I catch myself smiling at my phone just because your name appears on the screen.

And yes, I wait for your texts. I check my phone more often than I should, hoping to see a message from you. Even an ordinary conversation with you can turn my day around.

I love seeing your face on video calls. Sometimes I don't even need a conversation about anything extraordinary. Just seeing you, looking at you, listening to you talk... somehow that is enough to make me happy.

And I have to admit, you give me butterflies in ways that are not always innocent. You cross my mind in the sweetest, most distracting ways. Sometimes I just wish you were here, close enough for me to hold you. I imagine holding your hands, looking at you while holding them, and forgetting for a little while that the rest of the world even exists. I don't know why that simple thought makes me so happy, but it does.

I know I can be annoying. I overthink, I talk a lot, and sometimes I probably make a big deal out of the smallest things. But whatever I feel, I feel it genuinely. I may not always know how to express myself perfectly, but I hope you can see the affection behind my words and all the little ways I try to show that I care.

I don't want to rush anything or put any expectations on you. I would rather let things unfold naturally, one conversation and one little moment at a time. I just hope that, as you get to know me, you will accept all the little parts of me too — the talkative one, the overthinker, the silly one, and the one who smiles a little too much because of you.

I hope we get to make beautiful memories together someday. Until then, keep being you, keep chasing everything you dream of, and please take care of yourself. I hope life is gentle with you and gives you all the happiness you deserve.

And if you ever wonder whether someone is quietly rooting for you, just know that I am. More than you probably realise.

With all my warmth,
Your girl, perhaps? ♡
